# Story template

One five-page arc, filled from the snippets in `snippets.json`. Each page is
narration text (with Eleven v3/v4 audio tags) plus `sfx:` for the sound-effect
sting that plays after it.

Slots: `{hero}`, `{hero_catchphrase}`, `{hero_trick}`, `{hero_party}`,
`{place}`, `{place_line}`, `{place_noise}`, `{place_culprit}`,
`{sidekick}`, `{sidekick_scared}`, `{sidekick_happy}`.

Arc: arrive, a strange noise, the sidekick gets scared, the hero's trick saves
the day, everyone has a Halloween party.

---

## Page 1: arrive

[warmly] Once upon a Halloween night, {hero} and {sidekick} went exploring in the {place}. {place_line}

sfx: {place_arrive_sfx}

## Page 2: a strange noise

[whispers] They tiptoed further in, step by step. Then, from somewhere in the dark... [pause] {place_noise}
[excited] "Who's there?" said {hero}.

sfx: {place_noise_sfx}

## Page 3: the sidekick gets scared

{sidekick} {sidekick_scared}.
[nervously] "I don't like this one bit," whispered {sidekick}.
[gently] "Don't worry," said {hero}. "I know just what to do."

sfx: {sidekick_scared_sfx}

## Page 4: the trick saves the day

[excited] "{hero_catchphrase}" shouted {hero}, and {hero_trick}.
And there, making all that noise, was {place_culprit}! [laughs]

sfx: {hero_trick_sfx}

## Page 5: the Halloween party

[happily] It was a Halloween party, and everyone was invited!
{sidekick} {sidekick_happy}, and {hero} {hero_party}.
[warmly] Happy Halloween, everybody. [whispers] The end.

sfx: {place_party_sfx}
